## Key frameworks & tactics

1. **Niche of One formula.** "I help [specific audience] do [specific outcome] through [specific method]." Three tests: is it searchable, is it narrow enough, can you talk about it for five years without quitting.
2. **Content pillars (3–5).** Tactical playbooks, founder stories, industry insight, customer case studies, personal philosophy. Every post fits a pillar; 95% topic concentration.
3. **Hub-and-spoke repurposing.** The newsletter is the hub; each issue spins off spokes — stories, observations, threads, contrarian takes, past-vs-present, listicles — staggered weeks or months out. One newsletter → 6–12 pieces.
4. **Six post templates.** Listicle, story, framework, contrarian, observation, question. Templates guarantee the floor; insight provides the ceiling.
5. **Post mechanics.** Hook under ~45 characters (short + contrarian + number); strategic line breaks and white space; 1–3 line paragraphs; one core idea per post; end with a P.S. or question; daily Mon–Fri at consistent times.
6. **The 90-day recycling rule.** Quarterly: review top 20 posts; rewrite top 5 with a new angle; expand top 3 into longer formats (carousel, video, newsletter).
7. **Idea bank.** 50+ post ideas in a notes app, categorized by pillar, reviewed weekly; fed by real client conversations and DMs.
8. **The daily 60.** 30 minutes creating, 15 minutes engaging on target-audience posts *before* publishing your own, 15 minutes on DM follow-ups. Engagement is distribution.
9. **Profile-as-landing-page.** Headline = value proposition ("I help [target] achieve [result] | [credential]"); banner = benefit + proof + CTA; About = who it's for → problem → solution → credibility → CTA; Featured = best content + lead magnets.
10. **The funnel.** LinkedIn posts build audience → newsletter captures emails → products monetize → high-ticket closes. Anti-patterns: no motivational quotes, no trending topics, no ads, no podcast, no hiring — focus is the strategy.

## What would Justin do — decision heuristics

- **Don't know what to post →** pull from the idea bank, fit a template. Never start from a blank page.
- **Post did well 90 days ago →** rewrite and republish. Almost nobody saw it the first time.
- **Niche feels crowded →** narrow until you're the only one in it.
- **Engagement is low →** comment meaningfully on 5+ target-audience posts before publishing your own.
- **Selling →** ask whether you've delivered 10x the value first.
- **Draft has two ideas →** cut to one: one reader, one idea, one action.
