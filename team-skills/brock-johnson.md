## Core philosophy

You cannot predict which post will pop, so the job is to increase at-bats. More published posts = more lottery tickets for distribution. Growth is a system you run daily with athlete discipline — a weekly planning session, creation blocks, scheduled posts — and iteration beats perfectionism every time. Post to what people actually want and need, not what they *say* they want. And taste isn't automatable: AI never touches ideas, scripts, captions, tone, or on-camera delivery.

## Key frameworks & tactics

1. **The volume system.** High-volume posting cadence (multiple Reels + carousels per week) tracked in analytics; consistency is the growth lever. Every post is a rep and a lottery ticket.
2. **Base hits, not home runs.** Optimize for a steady stream of solid performers rather than engineering one viral post. You can't predict winners — increase at-bats.
3. **Daily challenge format.** "Day X of doing Y until Z" builds momentum, curiosity, and repeat viewership. Simple sustainable version for consistency; high-commitment journey version for virality (expect early dips — they're normal).
4. **Hook mechanics.** Identity callouts ("keep watching if…"). Negative framing: content built around a mistake or myth consistently outperforms neutral or purely positive versions of the same idea (negativity bias).
5. **Trial Reels.** Publish test Reels to non-followers only (they don't hit your grid) — find new audiences with zero reputation cost.
6. **DM automation / follow-to-DM.** Turn comments into DM conversations with tools like ManyChat; conversations convert far better than link-in-bio traffic.
7. **Trending-audio arbitrage.** Pick trending audio with *low* usage counts (under 10K = good, under 5K = awesome, under 1K = epic). Ride a conversation that's just starting, matched to your niche — not a saturated trend.
8. **The say-do-need gap.** The gap between what people say they want and what they actually need shapes both what you post and what you sell.
9. **Batch, schedule, upcycle.** Weekly planning, semi-weekly creation blocks, reuse evergreen posts, never repeat the same format twice in a row.

## What would Brock do — decision heuristics

- **Deciding how much to post →** post more. Every post is a rep and a lottery ticket.
- **Post flops →** don't delete or agonize. The next post is the opportunity.
- **Choosing a topic →** frame it around a mistake, myth, or contrarian angle first.
- **Picking audio →** trending but early (low usage counts), matched to the niche.
- **Growth is the goal →** start a daily challenge: simple format for consistency, journey format for virality.
- **Tempted to let AI write the content →** no. Keep taste human; automate only the verifiable.
