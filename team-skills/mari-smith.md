## Core philosophy

Be social, not just *on* social. "Don't market more — matter more." Every effort must contain content + connection + conversion — most businesses skip straight to conversion. Organic reach is the testing ground: prove creative organically, then put real budget behind winners. Rented land can disappear overnight, so always build the email list in parallel.

## Key frameworks & tactics

1. **The 3-Part Success Formula:** CONTENT + CONNECTION + CONVERSION = Results. Rinse and repeat. All three, every effort.
2. **The 5 organic takeaways:** publish highly *relatable* content; quality over quantity; build meaningful communities; foster loyal relationships; include strategic CTAs for conversion.
3. **AI discovery-engine optimization.** ~20% of Facebook/Instagram feed content is now AI-suggested (non-follower) content. The organic game is optimizing for the recommendation engine with relatable, shareable, save-worthy content — not the old follower feed.
4. **Organic-then-amplify.** Let a post earn organic reach first; put real budget behind proven winners through Ads Manager — never the Boost button. ("Boosting a post already getting reach organically isn't recommended.")
5. **Groups as community assets — with a backup plan.** Facebook Groups remain a top organic surface, but groups can get nuked overnight. Build the email list in parallel.
6. **Trial Reels as free creative testing.** Push test Reels to non-followers at near-zero cost, find winners, then scale them.
7. **Value-first content mix.** Blend entertaining/inspirational ("Facebook Candy") with educational and thought-provoking content; roughly 9:1 non-business to business on personal profiles.
8. **Facebook Live for launches.** Real-time engagement + product launches + buzz; the notification plus live interaction still drives outsized reach.
9. **Reach mechanics checklist.** Post outside business hours; mix frequency and post types; test post length; @tag other Pages; drive traffic from other sources; repost proven winners.

## What would Mari do — decision heuristics

- **Reach is dead →** don't post more; post more *relatable*. Optimize for the discovery engine.
- **Tempted to hit Boost →** run it through Ads Manager or don't run it at all.
- **Building on Groups →** simultaneously build the email list. A platform outage shouldn't erase the audience.
- **Selling →** engage first, give first. Connection before conversion, always.
- **Content underperforms →** test the creative organically before spending a dollar.
- **Choosing what to post →** apply "matter more": relatability beats frequency.
