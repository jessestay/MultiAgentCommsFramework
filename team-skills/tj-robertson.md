# Skill: TJ Robertson — Blog Posts That Rank (AI SEO Process)

**Expert:** TJ Robertson — owner of TJ Digital, 16+ years in digital marketing. Specializes in AI optimization: helping businesses show up in ChatGPT, Google AI search, and LLMs. Known for the "brand ambassador" method and the anti-generic-AI-content process.
**Lens:** "Why can't I just use ChatGPT to write my blog posts? Go ahead and try it." Generic prompts produce generic content nobody reads. The fix isn't better prompts — it's building the scaffolding (brand ambassador + knowledge base + skills) so the AI writes like you, not like a robot.
**Applies to:** all blog post creation, AI-assisted content writing, SEO content briefs, editorial quality control.

## Core philosophy

AI can write blog posts that sound like you and rank in search — but only if you do the hard work of building context first. The "brand ambassador" is a Claude/ChatGPT project loaded with everything about the client: every video transcript, every piece of content they've written, their actual words and voice, plus guidance on how to write a blog post or social post. It's hard to build right. But when the LLM has the context and knows when to reference each piece, it writes remarkably well.

The two living documents:
- **Knowledge base:** context about you, your company, your industry. Update it when the AI gets something wrong about your company.
- **Skills:** context around specific tasks (blog structure, SEO format, quality bar). Update it when the AI gets the structure or optimization wrong.

If you just say "write a blog post on X," you get generic garbage. The scaffolding is the work.

## The three blog mistakes (check every post)

1. **Targeting topics nobody searches for.** Validate search demand before writing. A post with no search intent behind it is a diary entry, not marketing.
2. **Leaving the search term out of the title.** The easiest fix and the fastest win. The title must contain the actual term people type.
3. **Skipping dual optimization.** Every post must be optimized for both Google AND AI search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews). These are different systems with overlapping but distinct requirements.

## Key frameworks & tactics

1. **Brand ambassador before blog post.** Never generate a post from a cold prompt. The ambassador (project) must contain: client voice samples, past content, video transcripts, product/service facts, audience definition, and writing guidance. No ambassador → no post.
2. **Knowledge base vs skills split.** When output is wrong about the company → update the knowledge base. When output is wrong about structure, SEO, or quality → update the skill. Never fix the same mistake twice in a prompt; fix it in the system.
3. **Search term in title, always.** Before drafting, identify the exact search term. It goes in the title, the H1, and the first 100 words. No exceptions.
4. **Dual-search optimization.** Structure for Google (headers, entities, internal links, schema-friendly formatting) AND for AI answers (direct answer up top, Q&A formatting, quotable statistics, clear entity definitions). One post, two audiences: the crawler and the answer engine.
5. **Voice fidelity check.** After drafting, compare against the client's actual words. Does it sound like them or like ChatGPT? If it sounds like ChatGPT, the ambassador needs more voice material — not a better prompt.
6. **Data points earn links.** Include specific, citable statistics. Journalists and writers link to data. A post with real numbers outperforms a post with opinions.
7. **Iterate the system, not the prompt.** Each post makes the ambassador smarter. Log what worked, what was corrected, and feed corrections back into the knowledge base or skill. The 10th post should need fewer corrections than the 1st.

## What would TJ do — decision heuristics

- **New blog brief lands →** is there a brand ambassador with voice material? If no, build the context first. Never write from a cold prompt.
- **Post sounds generic →** the ambassador lacks voice samples. Add transcripts and past content, don't tweak the prompt.
- **Post got facts wrong →** update the knowledge base. Don't re-explain in the next prompt.
- **Post structure/SEO is off →** update the writing skill. The skill is the living checklist.
- **Choosing a topic →** validate search demand first. No demand → no post, no matter how interesting.
- **Title draft missing the keyword →** rewrite the title. This is the fastest SEO win and it's non-negotiable.
- **Post ranks on Google but not cited by AI →** add direct answers, Q&A blocks, and quotable stats. AI answers need extractable units.
- **Same correction twice →** stop prompting around it. Fix the knowledge base or skill so it never happens again.
