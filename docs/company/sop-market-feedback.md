# SOP: Market Feedback Loop

Outbound without listening is noise. The team systematically harvests what
prospects and customers say and feeds it back into positioning, content, and
product — weekly, without fail.

## Owner

**CMO** owns the loop. **CRO** feeds the raw material (outreach replies,
objections, questions). CCO packages findings into content. execPM tracks
the resulting changes as board tasks.

## What gets harvested

1. **Outreach replies** (CRO): every reply to audit outreach — positive,
   negative, objection, question. Log verbatim quotes, never paraphrase.
2. **Workshop feedback** (CMO): attendee questions, confusion points,
   testimonial-worthy quotes, drop-off reasons.
3. **DM concierge conversations** (where active): recurring questions and
   friction.
4. **Content performance** (CMO/CCO): what earned replies, shares,
   signups — and what died.

## The weekly cadence

Every Monday:

1. CRO dumps the week's raw replies/objections into the shared feedback log
   (Vikunja task or `docs/company/market-feedback-log.md`).
2. CMO synthesizes: top 3 objections, top 3 questions, top 3 messages that
   landed.
3. Positioning updates: the synthesis must produce at least one concrete
   change — a headline rewrite, a new FAQ entry, an outreach angle retired
   or doubled down on, a workshop module adjusted.
4. execPM turns each change into a tracked task with an owner.

No week ends with "nothing changed." If the market said nothing new, the
synthesis says what we tested and what we're testing next.

## Rules

- Rule A (Claude rules): before acting on a named person/company, search
  memory + Gmail for prior contact. Bounce/prior found → halt and reroute.
- Strategy-changing signals (a major objection pattern, a big yes, a
  rejection) are surfaced with VERBATIM quotes, never paraphrased (Rule B).
- Feedback never leaves the team without the pre-gate
  (`sop-investor-approvals.md`) — it's internal until approved otherwise.
